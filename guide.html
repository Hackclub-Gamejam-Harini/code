A player's guide

How to Brew for
Strange Customers

Frogs, satyrs, and whoever else wanders in want their drink made right — and fast. Here's everything you need to know before your first order comes in.

Your station

Everything on the counter can be dragged. Some things pour, some things just get carried to the cup.

Kettle & coffee pot

Drag one over the cup, then hold it there and tap the arrow keys to tip it. Tip past a steep angle to pour.

Milk carton

Same trick — drag it to the cup and tip it with the arrow keys. It only pours once there's something for it to swirl into.

Raspberry & lemon bowls

No tipping needed — grab a piece straight from the bowl and drop it directly onto the cup.

The cup

Drag it anywhere on the counter to line it up under whatever you're pouring or dropping ingredients into.

Pouring a drink

Liquids all work the same way — it's the tilt that triggers the pour.

  1. Line it up

    Drag the kettle, coffee pot, or milk carton so it overlaps the cup.

  2. Keep holding, then tip

    Without letting go, press ← or → repeatedly. Each press tilts the container a little further.

  3. Watch the cup fill

    Once it's tipped far enough, the pour happens automatically and the cup's contents update.

  4. Add anything solid last

    Pull a raspberry or lemon slice from its bowl and drop it onto the cup — no tilting required.

Tip — if a pour doesn't seem to take, check what's already sitting in the cup. Some ingredients only settle in on top of another.

The order book

Read the order card in the top-left. Match its ingredient list exactly before you serve.

Serving up

The clock

Every customer gives you 20 seconds. Let it run out and you'll lose the order automatically.

Serve button

Once the cup matches the order, press Serve. Get it wrong and the customer walks away unhappy.

Score

Correct orders pay out by how many ingredients they needed — more complex drinks, more reward.
