# The Roots Cafe

This game is about serving customers in a nice and cozy cafe


# A player's guide

## How to Brew for Strange Customers
Frogs, satyrs, and whoever else wanders in want their drink made right — and fast. Here's everything you need to know before your first order comes in.

### Your station
Everything on the counter can be dragged. Some things pour, some things just get carried to the cup.

### Kettle & coffee pot
Drag one over the cup, then hold it there and tap the arrow keys to tip it. Tip past a steep angle to pour.

### Milk carton
Same trick — drag it to the cup and tip it with the arrow keys. It only pours once there's something for it to swirl into.

### Raspberry & lemon bowls
No tipping needed — grab a piece straight from the bowl and drop it directly onto the cup.

### The cup
Drag it anywhere on the counter to line it up under whatever you're pouring or dropping ingredients into.
